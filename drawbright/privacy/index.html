Privacy · Drawbright

We don't collect any data. Drawbright has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or app backend, and it makes no network connections of its own. It is made for children and adults, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your drawings, lesson stars, practice scores, certificates, chosen alphabet and the name you type for certificates are stored only on your device. We can't see them. Device backups may include this data. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Speech: letters and lesson names are spoken with the device's built-in voice, on the device.

Photos: Drawbright asks for permission only to save a drawing you export to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Sharing: when you share a drawing, video or certificate, the iOS share sheet sends it only where you choose. That destination has its own privacy practices.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details; Apple tells the app only whether Drawbright Plus is active.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to serve and secure the website. This is separate from the app. If you email us, we receive the message and the information you choose to include, and use it only to reply.
