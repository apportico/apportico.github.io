Support · Drawbright

Drawing lessons

Open a world, pick a lesson and trace the coloured line, starting at the green dot. Tap Show Me to see the line drawn first, and Undo to try a line again. Tap the small picture at the top to see the finished drawing big. When you finish, your drawing opens on the canvas so you can colour it in; it is saved in Drawings.

Stars and levels

Each lesson gives up to 3 stars. The first 3 lessons of every world are open right away; each later lesson opens when the one before has a star. Earn a star on every lesson in a world for a bronze certificate, two stars for silver and three for gold. Put your name on certificates in Settings.

Letters and numbers

Open the ABC world and choose an alphabet from the menu at the top: English, Russian, Arabic, Armenian, German, French, Spanish or Italian. Letters are said aloud in their language using the device's built-in voice (Armenian has no voice on Apple devices, so it is silent). Turn sound off in Settings, or use the silent switch.

Drawbright Plus

Drawbright Plus unlocks every lesson, world and practice exercise: monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time unlock. Family Sharing is supported. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → Restore Purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

Moving to a new device

Drawings and progress are stored on the device and included in your device backup. To keep a drawing elsewhere, open it and use Share to export a PNG or time-lapse video.
