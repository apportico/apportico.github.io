Midnight Bakery Support

Playing a shift

Swipe a tray or tap an arrow to move it one space. Pastries fall toward the labeled order boxes. Fill every order to complete the puzzle. Tap Rules on a board to see how its shelves, switches, or other features work.

Stars and the daily puzzle

Earn three stars at the displayed move target, two within two extra moves, or one for any other clear. Your best score stays saved after a replay. Open Shifts to find the daily puzzle and the Game Center leaderboards.

Purchases and audio

Expert Shifts is a one-time purchase. To recover it, open Shifts, scroll to Full campaign, and tap Restore purchase while using the Apple Account that bought it. Music and sound effects have separate switches in the speaker menu.

Contact

Email Midnight Bakery support. Include the app version, device model, and shift name if you are reporting a puzzle issue.
