Midnight Bakery Privacy Policy

Apportico does not run analytics, serve ads, or operate a gameplay server or player account system for Midnight Bakery.

On your device

Midnight Bakery saves puzzle progress, move records, audio settings, and Game Center submission state on your device. If you connect Game Center, the app saves the Game Center player identifier locally to keep your progress associated with the same account.

Apple services

Apple handles in-app purchases and restoration. If you choose to use Apple Game Center, the game reports leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple. Game Center rankings and profile visibility follow your Apple settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Contact
