Privacy · Whenlet

Everything you enter in Whenlet (things, dates, notes and categories) is saved on this device. We do not collect app data or operate an app backend. There is no account, advertising, analytics or network access in the app. Recent automatic snapshots are also kept on the device. Device backups may include app data. Exporting a backup sends only the file you create to the destination you select; that destination has its own privacy practices. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to serve and secure the website. This is separate from the app. If you email support, we receive the message and information you choose to include. Do not send passwords or private backup contents. We use support correspondence to respond to your request. For privacy questions, contact us at the address below.
