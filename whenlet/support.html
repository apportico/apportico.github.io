Support · Whenlet

Add a thing, choose One time or Repeats, and say when you last did it: now, on a date, or “I don't know”. Whenlet never invents a date. Tap Done when you do it again; Undo appears straight after. Up next shows what is left; things you have done step aside until they are due again, and All shows everything.

Open a thing to see its history. Log an earlier time, edit a date or note, or delete a mistake. Categories can be added, renamed, reordered and removed in Settings.

To move to a new device, use Settings → Export backup, then Settings → Import on the new device. Import checks the whole file first and changes nothing if it cannot be read. Settings also keeps recent automatic snapshots you can restore.

Intervals and deadlines are visual check-ins only. Whenlet does not send notifications and is not meant for medical, medication or other safety-critical tracking.
