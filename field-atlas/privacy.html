Field Atlas Privacy Policy

Field Atlas is provided by Daniele Zanni. The app stores your journal and preferences on your device. Field Atlas has no developer account sign-in, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Your journal and photos

Your journal can contain travel dates, places, notes, and photos. When you attach photos, Field Atlas imports copies of only the items you select in the system photo picker. Field Atlas uses journal information to show your maps, trips, and configured widgets. When you share a postcard or export a backup, the app passes the file to the destination you select.

Location

Field Atlas requests your location when you use a nearby-site search or choose to use your current position on a map. The app uses it on your device for distance sorting or map positioning. If you save a pin at your location, those coordinates become part of the journal.

Optional Apple services

When you enable iCloud Sync or create an iCloud backup, your journal and photos are stored in your private iCloud account through Apple. You can control these features in the app and manage iCloud data using Apple’s settings.

Apple handles in-app payments and restores. Field Atlas checks your purchase entitlement with StoreKit to unlock features. We do not receive payment card details.

If you use Game Center to sync achievements, Field Atlas sends the earned achievement identifiers and progress to Apple. Each time you choose Share score in Travel stats, Field Atlas sends the total for the selected leaderboard to Apple. The total is a count of countries or UNESCO sites. Other players may see your best shared score and Game Center profile. Field Atlas keeps trip names, individual places, coordinates, dates, photos, and notes out of score submissions. See Apple’s Game Center privacy notice for information about your Game Center data and controls.

Support

If you email support, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please keep journal backups and account credentials out of ordinary support emails. Contact daniele@duck.com with privacy questions.
