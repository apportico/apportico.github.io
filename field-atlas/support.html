Field Atlas Support

Build your travel journal

Choose Add a trip in the Journal. Search for countries, cities, or UNESCO World Heritage sites. Tap a place in the trip editor to add dates or photos, move it in the itinerary, or remove it.

Back up or sync your journal

Open Settings in Field Atlas. Export a backup to Files, or use iCloud Backup to save a copy to your Apple Account. To keep journals in sync, enable iCloud Sync on the devices you want to use with the same Apple Account.

Restore a purchase

Open Map styles or Trip Widgets in Field Atlas Settings and choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account you used to make the purchase.

Contact support

Email Field Atlas support with your device, app version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Keep private trip details and account credentials out of your message.
