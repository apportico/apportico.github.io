Ink & Block Privacy Policy

Ink & Block keeps your puzzle progress on your device. The app has no Apportico account, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Apple handles purchases and restores through StoreKit. It tells the app whether Full Game is unlocked. Apportico does not receive your payment details.

If you use Game Center, the app sends achievement progress to Apple. The app uses your Game Center player identifier on the device to report progress to the correct account. See Apple’s privacy policy.

If you email support, we use the information you send to answer you.
