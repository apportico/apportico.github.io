Ink & Block Support

How to play

Choose a block, drag it into place, and use Rotate when it needs to turn. Tap Print to add its shape. Match the target by layering the blocks in the right order. Use Undo to try a different placement.

Restore Full Game

Open Album and choose a chapter after Market Day. Tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought Full Game. The purchase unlocks 100 puzzles across five chapters.

Game Center

Tap the trophy beside Album to see your achievements. Ink & Block saves finished puzzles on your device and reports achievement progress when Game Center is available.

Contact

Email Ink & Block support with your app version, iPhone model, and what happened.
