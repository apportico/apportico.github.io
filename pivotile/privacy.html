Privacy · Pivotile

Pivotile saves your current game, best score, statistics and settings on this device. We do not collect app data or operate an app backend. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or network access in the app. Device backups may include app data. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; device backups must be managed separately.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to serve and secure the website. This is separate from the app. If you email support, we receive the message and information you choose to include, and use it only to respond to your request.
