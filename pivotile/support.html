Support · Pivotile

Drag a piece from the tray onto the 9 × 9 board. Prefer tapping? Tap a piece, tap the board to preview it, then tap the same spot again to place it. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. When all three pieces are placed, three new ones arrive.

Pivots let you turn a waiting piece by 90°. You start each game with one pivot. You earn another by clearing two or more lines with one piece, or by clearing lines three times in a row. You can hold up to three; tap ↻ under a piece to spend one. The game ends only when no piece fits, even after the pivots you have.

Every new set of pieces contains at least one piece that fits somewhere, when any space allows it. Otherwise pieces are random; the game does not adjust difficulty to end your run.

Your game is saved after every move and continues when you reopen the app. Settings has haptics, a calmer celebration option, the tutorial, and a reset for your best score and statistics. Pivotile works offline and has no ads or account.
