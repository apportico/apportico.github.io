Puppy Hours support

Email Puppy Hours support with your app version, device model, and what happened.

Set up app blocking

Open Settings in Puppy Hours and choose Distraction controls. Follow the Screen Time prompt on your iPhone, then choose a blocking mode before starting a timed session. You can change or revoke access in iOS Settings.

Focus with friends

Open Settings in Puppy Hours and choose Focus with friends. Give the app local network access, then host or join a room on a nearby device. Leave the room when you’re finished.

Restore or manage Plus

Open Puppy Hours Plus from the app’s settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally made the purchase. For billing or cancellation, open your subscriptions in Apple Account settings.

Back up your focus data

Use Settings, then Local data, to export a backup. On the other device, open Local data to import it. Keep the backup private if it includes personal tasks or focus history.
