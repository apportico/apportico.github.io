Puppy Hours privacy policy

Puppy Hours is published by Daniele Zanni. You can use the focus timer without creating an account. The app has no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics.

Data on your device

Puppy Hours saves your puppy names, tasks, focus history, rewards, preferences, and optional room photos and greetings on your device. The widget and Watch app use focus information to show your timer. You can export a copy of your focus data or clear local focus history and tasks in Settings under Local data.

Notifications run on your device. Calendar and Reminders access is optional. The app uses the items you choose to import and writes sessions to Calendar only when you choose to export them.

Nearby focus rooms

If you host or join a room, Puppy Hours uses the local network and a direct, encrypted connection between participating devices. People in the room can see the participating puppies’ names, breeds, and shared timer state. Leave the room to end your connection.

Screen Time

If you grant Screen Time permission, Puppy Hours uses your app and website selections to apply the restrictions you choose during a timed focus session. Puppy Hours does not send those selections to the developer or use Screen Time data for advertising. You can revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

Purchases and support

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions. Puppy Hours checks the resulting entitlement through StoreKit to unlock Plus and restore purchases. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account settings. Apple’s privacy policy

If you email support, the developer uses your email address and the information you choose to send to answer your request. Ask a privacy question
