Night Garden support

Reminders

Open your mouse’s tab, then Garden tools and Habit reminders. Choose a habit and set its reminder time. If reminders are off for the app, open iPhone or iPad Settings and allow notifications for Night Garden.

Home Screen widget

Press an empty area of the Home Screen until the icons move. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Night Garden and choose the small or medium size. The widget opens Home when tapped.

Moving your garden to another device

In Night Garden, open Settings, then Garden data. Use Export garden to save a backup. In Night Garden on your other device, use Import garden and review what will be merged. The export can include your private notes and habit history.

When iCloud sync is enabled, devices signed in to the same Apple Account can sync the private garden data. The app shows the sync status in Settings.

Get in touch

Email Night Garden support with the app version, device model and a short description of the issue. Keep private notes and garden exports out of your message unless they are needed to investigate.
