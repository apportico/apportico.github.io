Night Garden privacy policy

Night Garden is published by Daniele Zanni. Questions about this policy can be sent to Night Garden support.

What stays on your device

Night Garden stores the garden, habit schedule and history, notes, check-ins, and preferences on your device. It uses optional setup answers to suggest habits. The app discards those answers when setup ends. The app has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable sync in Night Garden settings, the app saves and reads your garden data in the private CloudKit storage tied to your Apple Account. The synced garden file can contain habit history and private notes. Night Garden’s developer does not receive access to your private CloudKit storage. Apple handles iCloud under its own privacy policy.

Turning off sync stops further syncing from that installation. Garden data already saved to iCloud may remain there. Apple privacy policy

Reminders, widgets and exports

Scheduled habit and outing reminders are local notifications controlled by your device settings. The Home Screen widget reads the mouse artwork and a short garden update from storage shared with Night Garden on your device.

If you export your garden, the file may contain private notes and habit history. You choose where to save or share that file. A shared discovery postcard contains the artwork and discovery title.

Contact

If you email support, your address and the message you send are used to answer your request. You can ask a privacy question without sending your habits, notes or exported garden file.
