How to play

Tap a gutter or splitter to turn it, then tap Let it rain. Splitters alternate between their two openings. The little drop marks where the next drop will go. Give each pot exactly the number of drops it needs.

Restore Full Game

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought the unlock. Full Game adds 100 gardens to the 12 free ones.
