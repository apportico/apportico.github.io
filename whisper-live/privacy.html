Privacy · Whisper Live

Whisper Live turns speech into text on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition, on-device Apple Intelligence (for optional clean-up) and on-device translation. Your voice and text are not sent to us or to any server. We do not collect app data or operate an app backend. There is no account, advertising, analytics or tracking in the app.

The microphone is used only while you dictate. Recordings made on Apple Watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone to be turned into text, and are not kept after that. Your history, settings, vocabulary and snippets are saved on your device. The keyboard and the app on the same device exchange text through the device's secure storage; this is why the keyboard asks for Full Access. Device backups may include app data. Exporting a backup sends only the file you create to the destination you choose; that destination has its own privacy practices. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

The first time you use a language, iOS may download Apple's speech or translation model for it. That download is handled by Apple and contains no recordings or text from you.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to serve and secure the website. This is separate from the app. If you email support, we receive the message and information you choose to include. We use support correspondence to respond to your request.
