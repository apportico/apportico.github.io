Whisper Live

Talk, and your words appear in any app. Whisper Live is a voice keyboard and dictation app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Speech is turned into text on your device, so it works offline.

  • Voice keyboard: tap the mic in Mail, Notes, Messages or any app and your words are typed as you speak.
  • Tuning for your voice: sensitivity, higher or lower voice, noise reduction, whisper mode and auto-stop after a pause.
  • Clean-up: removes filler words, fixes punctuation, voice commands like "new line" and "delete that", optional on-device AI polish and writing styles.
  • Your words: custom vocabulary, replacements and snippets.
  • Translate as you dictate, with Apple's on-device translation.
  • Apple Watch: record on your wrist, and your iPhone turns it into text.

Free: 20 dictations a day and the built-in templates (To-do list, Meeting notes, Key points, Social post). Whisper Live Pro – $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year – adds unlimited dictation and your own templates. Cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.
