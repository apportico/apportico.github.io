Launchlark Privacy Policy

On your device

Launchlark stores the App Store Connect private key you choose to import in the device’s Keychain. Account settings and reports are stored locally. Launchlark has no separate sign-in.

Requests to Apple

When you connect an account or refresh, Launchlark sends authenticated requests directly to Apple. Apple supplies your app list, sales and analytics reports, and written reviews when requested. Launchlark also retrieves app artwork from Apple. Apple’s processing is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Data collection

Launchlark does not send your private key, app reports, or reviews to an Apportico server. The app has no advertising or third-party tracking. Sample mode uses fictional data included in the app.

Remove your data

Open Accounts and choose Disconnect to remove your local key, profile, and report cache. To revoke a key at Apple, use App Store Connect. Files you imported or copied separately can be removed in Files.
