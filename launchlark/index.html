Launchlark Support

Launchlark brings together your App Store downloads, proceeds and analytics.

Try it with sample data

Tap Explore sample data on the welcome screen. Choose Exit sample to return and connect your own apps.

Connect your own apps

Use a team Sales and Reports key from App Store Connect. Import the key file from Files and enter the Key ID and Issuer ID. For Sales & Trends, enter the vendor number shown in Payments and Financial Reports → Reports.

See page views and impressions

Open Analytics. If an app needs setup, follow the Apple guide shown in the app and ask your App Store Connect Admin to request ongoing reports. After setup, select Check setup. Apple typically generates the first reports within 24–48 hours.

Contact

Email Launchlark support. Please keep private keys and raw reports out of support requests.
