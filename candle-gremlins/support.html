Candle Gremlins: Haunted Manor Support

Night Shift

Open the room map and choose a locked thumbnail in the scrollable list on the left to find Night Shift. It unlocks 100 additional rooms with one purchase. Tap the price to unlock the rooms. If the price is unavailable, tap the circular arrow around the price tag to retry. If you already own Night Shift, tap the receipt with the return arrow to restore your purchase using the purchasing Apple Account.

Scores and achievements

On the home screen, tap the medal to see achievements from your saved room progress, including offline. Tap the trophy to browse the Game Center leaderboards. In the room map, tap the trophy to open the board for the selected campaign. Manor Stars covers 20 rooms, and Night Shift Stars covers the 100 expansion rooms. Game Center sign-in is optional. You can play rooms and save your best stars on your iPhone without signing in.

Contact

Email Apportico support with the app version, iPhone model and room number, if relevant. Avoid sending account or payment details.
