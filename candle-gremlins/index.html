Candle Gremlins: Haunted Manor

Pull back and fling household objects to knock out candle gremlins in a haunted manor. Tap midflight to unleash each object’s power.

The upcoming iPhone game includes 20 free rooms. A one-time Night Shift purchase unlocks 100 more. Optional Game Center keeps track of achievements and your best stars on separate Manor and Night Shift leaderboards.
