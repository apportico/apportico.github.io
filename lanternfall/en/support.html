Lanternfall support

How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to move. Your lantern fights on its own. Gather embers to level up, then choose a new weapon, charm or upgrade. Survive until dawn to win the night.

Full Game and Restore Purchases

The first night, two keepers and four weapons are free. The one-time Full Game purchase unlocks nine more nights, five more keepers, four more weapons, twenty-four more relics and Darker Nights. To restore it, open Full Game and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Progress

Glowstones, upgrades and opened nights are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes this progress.

Email Lanternfall support. Include your app version, iPhone model and the night you played.
