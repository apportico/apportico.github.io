Privacy · Tumblepatch

Tumblepatch is made for young children and collects no data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party code, and it does not use the camera, microphone or location. Puzzle progress and settings are saved only on this device. Device backups may include them. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

The only network use is Apple's In-App Purchase system for the optional one-time unlock. Apple handles the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not receive payment details or any information about the buyer. Purchases and links to this website sit behind a parental gate in the app.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to serve and secure the website. This is separate from the app. If you email support, we receive the message and information you choose to include and use it only to reply. For privacy questions, contact us at the address below.
