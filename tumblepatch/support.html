Support · Tumblepatch

Pick a group (cars, helper vehicles, trucks and buses, sky and space, sea, dinosaurs or animals), then a puzzle. Drag each piece from the tray to its shadow in the picture. A piece dropped in the wrong place simply slides back. When the picture is finished, tap it to make it drive, fly, sail or stomp. The arrow opens the next puzzle and the circle builds the same one again.

Settings are behind the lock button on the home screen. Answer the small multiplication question to open them. There you can turn sound and the hint button on or off, choose how many pieces your child places (3, 4, 6, 8 or 10) and start every puzzle over.

The first puzzle in each group is free. One payment unlocks every puzzle; there is no subscription. If you bought it before, open the settings, tap Unlock all puzzles, then Restore purchase. With Family Sharing, family members can unlock it too.

Progress is saved on the device. Deleting the app removes it.
