Snowball Yard Support

Roll and stack

Tap a ball to preview its moves. Swipe from the ball or tap an arrow. Roll onto fresh snow to grow one size. Tap a tile’s guide card for its rule.

Restore Full Game

Open the yard counter, choose Full Game, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Achievements

Tap the trophy to open your Game Center achievements. Sign in to Game Center in Settings if prompted.

Contact

Email Snowball Yard support with your app version, device model, and what happened.
