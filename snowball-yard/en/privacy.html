Snowball Yard Privacy

Snowball Yard keeps puzzle completion, the last selected yard, and sound settings on your device. It has no separate account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Apple handles the optional Full Game purchase and restores through the App Store. The app checks the verified purchase entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment details.

If you sign in to Game Center, the app sends achievement progress to Apple and uses the Game Center player identifier on the device to report to the correct account. Version 1.1 and later also save your best yard times locally and send best times and completed-yard totals to Apple when you use Game Center. See Apple’s privacy policy.

If you email support, we use the information you send to respond to you.
