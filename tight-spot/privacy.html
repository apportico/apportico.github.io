Tight Spot Privacy Policy

Tight Spot saves your parking progress, best runs, and sound setting on your device. We do not collect personal data through the app. The game has no developer account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Apple handles purchases and restores through the App Store. The app uses Apple’s purchase status to unlock Full Game. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Game Center is optional. When you connect, Tight Spot sends leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple. Your leaderboard entry can be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. See Game Center & Privacy.

If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond.
