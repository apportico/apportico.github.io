Tight Spot Support

Parking

Hold the pedal to move and slide your thumb left or right to steer. Lift your thumb to brake. Use the shifter to switch between drive and reverse. The bay shows which way to park. Fewer maneuvers and quicker clean runs earn more stars.

Full Game and restore

Tight Spot starts with 20 courses. The one-time Full Game purchase opens the other 100. To restore it, open Courses, tap any locked course, then tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account you bought it with.

Game Center

Open Records to connect to Game Center and view leaderboards and achievements. You can play the parking courses offline.

Contact

Email Tight Spot Support with your app version, device model, and what happened.
