Flag Detective

Identify countries from their flags, outlines, and capitals. Every clue you master fills in part of your atlas.

Daily rounds

Five daily rounds are available, with a new set at midnight UTC. Tap a region and then a country to study its flag, outline, and capital.

Unlock and restore

Unlock the World adds unlimited practice and themed challenges. To restore it, open a locked practice mode and choose Restore purchase while signed into the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Game Center

Tap the trophy on the atlas to see achievements and the Daily Atlas leaderboard. Game Center uses the Apple Account and privacy settings configured on your device.

Contact

Email Flag Detective support for help with a purchase, a clue, or the app.
