Privacy policy

Daniele Zanni publishes Flag Detective. The game saves quiz progress and your language choice on your device. The app has no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics.

Game Center

If you are signed into Game Center, the game reports achievement progress and daily leaderboard scores to Apple. Apple processes this information under its Game Center privacy notice. Your Game Center settings control score visibility.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases and restores. The app receives the entitlement status so it can unlock the relevant practice modes.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical information to deliver and protect the site. If you contact support, we use your message and contact information to respond. Contact us about privacy.
