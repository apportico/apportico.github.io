Pocket Poltergeist Support

Night Shift

Open the room map and tap the moon tab to find Night Shift. It unlocks 100 additional rooms with one purchase. If you already own it, tap Restore Purchases in the same panel using the purchasing Apple Account.

Game Center

On the home screen, tap the trophy for the leaderboard or the medal for achievements. Game Center sign-in is optional. You can play rooms and save your best stars on your iPhone without signing in.

Contact

Email Apportico support with the app version, iPhone model and room number, if relevant. Avoid sending account or payment details.
