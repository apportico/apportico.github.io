Pocket Poltergeist Privacy

Apportico does not require a separate account. Best room ratings, power tutorial progress and sound preferences are saved on your device. The game contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

If you use Game Center, the game submits your free-room star total and achievement progress to Apple under your Game Center account. Apple also processes Night Shift purchases and restores. See Apple’s privacy policy for details about these services.

If you email support, Apportico uses your email address and the information you provide to respond. Email Apportico with privacy questions.
