WalkLedger support

For app questions, feedback or help with a problem, contact daniele@duck.com.

Developer: Daniele Zanni.

WalkLedger is a project record app for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. It works without an account, app server or subscription. Your projects are stored locally on the device where you create them.

Start a project

Create a project and give it a name. Add a site or address if useful. Inside the project, add an entry and choose its type:

  • Issue: record work to follow up on, with optional location, text, assignee and due date. Mark it done when complete or reopen it later.
  • Note: keep observations and reference photos. Notes are counted separately and do not increase open or completed issue totals.

An assignee is a label for your records, not an invitation. Due dates do not send reminders. WalkLedger does not provide shared accounts, automatic messages or live collaboration.

Add photos and markup

Open an entry to select photos using the system photo picker or take a photo with the camera. Camera access is optional. If it is unavailable or denied, you can still choose existing photos.

Issues organize photos into Before and After. Notes show their photos together. Open a photo to add red markup, undo a stroke or clear the markup. Markup is stored separately from the original file saved in WalkLedger. The app does not overwrite your Photos library originals.

WalkLedger preserves the image file supplied by the system photo picker or camera. Photos may contain embedded information such as capture time or location. Keep that in mind when exporting a complete backup.

Share a PDF report

Open a project and use its Entries filter to select All entries, Open issues only or Completed issues. Then choose Share PDF. The report follows the current filter: All entries includes notes, while both issue-only choices exclude notes. It contains the entries in that scope, their details, photos and any markup.

Choose a recipient or destination in the system share sheet. A PDF is a readable report, not a restorable backup. Reports are working records, not certified evidence or a guarantee of inspection compliance.

Back up and move your work

In Settings → Backup & restore, choose Export complete backup. Save the .walkledger file outside the app in a place you trust. It includes every project, its entries, original photos and markup. Export a new backup whenever you need an up-to-date copy.

To restore, choose Restore backup, select your .walkledger file, review the counts and confirm. Restored projects are added as new copies. Existing projects are not overwritten or merged, and importing the same backup again creates more copies.

PDFs and backup files are not password-protected or encrypted by WalkLedger. Anyone who can access an exported file may be able to read its contents. Choose sharing and storage destinations carefully.

WalkLedger does not automatically sync across devices or keep a server copy. Your device's own backup settings may include app data. Deleting WalkLedger removes its local data. Before deleting the app, replacing your device or troubleshooting, save a complete backup outside the app and check that you can access it.

Troubleshooting

  • A photo will not import: the file may be unsupported, damaged or too large. Try another image and check available device storage. Photos held only in iCloud may need a connection for the system photo picker to retrieve them.
  • A backup will not restore: keep the original backup file, check free storage and make sure WalkLedger is up to date. The app checks backup contents before adding projects. A backup made with a newer unsupported format may require a newer app version.
  • Existing data cannot be loaded: do not delete the app. If WalkLedger reports that it has preserved the data and blocked changes, contact support with the message shown.
  • A report or backup will not export: free some device storage and try again. Large projects need space to prepare an export.
  • Camera access is off: review WalkLedger's camera permission in device Settings, or use Add photos instead.
  • Language or appearance: open WalkLedger Settings. Use the appearance controls or Open language settings to choose from the app languages available in device Settings.

Contact support safely

Email daniele@duck.com with your WalkLedger version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, the steps that led to the issue and the exact error message. You can find the app version in WalkLedger Settings.

Please do not send a full project, backup, original photo or private screenshot unless it is needed and you are comfortable sharing it. Remove personal or confidential information from examples. Support cannot retrieve projects from a WalkLedger server because there is no server copy.

For App Store billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support. WalkLedger has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
