WalkLedger privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

WalkLedger is provided by Daniele Zanni. For privacy questions, contact daniele@duck.com.

This policy describes the WalkLedger iPhone and iPad app. It also explains what happens when you choose to export your work, contact support or visit these support pages.

Data stays under your control

WalkLedger has no app backend, user accounts, advertising, tracking tools, third-party analytics SDKs or app-managed cloud sync. The app does not upload your projects, notes or photos to us. Its core recording, PDF generation and backup functions run on your device.

The app stores the information you enter or import in its local app storage. That can include project names, site or address labels, entry titles and text, issue status, assignee labels, due dates, photos, photo markup and record dates. Appearance preferences are also stored locally.

These records may contain personal information if you choose to add it. Only include information and photos that you have the right to use. We cannot view, retrieve or restore your local projects remotely.

Photos and camera

You can select images using the system photo picker. WalkLedger receives only the images you choose, rather than access to browse your entire photo library. You may also grant camera access to take photos for an entry. Camera access is optional and can be changed in device Settings.

WalkLedger stores the original image file delivered by the photo picker or camera. Red markup is stored separately, without changing that original. Image files can contain embedded metadata, including capture time or location, and original files are included in complete backups. WalkLedger does not use device location services to track you.

Reports, backups and sharing

When you request a PDF report or complete .walkledger backup, WalkLedger creates the file on your device. Export preparation and backup restoration can create temporary files in the app's storage.

A report contains the project and entry information included in the selected report scope, with photos and markup. A complete backup contains all projects and entries, original image files and markup. Restoring a backup adds new project copies without overwriting existing projects.

You decide where an exported file goes through the system share sheet or file picker. Choosing an email, messaging, cloud storage or other service sends the file to that recipient or service under its own terms and privacy practices. WalkLedger does not control those destinations or copies they retain.

WalkLedger does not encrypt or password-protect exported PDFs or backup files. Protect these files and share them only with people and services you trust. Backup integrity checks detect damaged data; they do not make a file authenticated, tamper-proof or certified evidence.

Device backups and Apple services

Your device backup settings may include WalkLedger's local data in backups managed by Apple or your computer. WalkLedger does not operate that backup service. If a selected image is stored in iCloud Photos, the system may download it before delivering it to the app.

Apple handles App Store purchases and may process device, purchase, diagnostic or usage information under Apple's settings and privacy policy. WalkLedger has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. These Apple services are separate from WalkLedger's local project storage.

Keeping and deleting information

Your active project records remain in local app storage until you delete them or delete the app. You can edit or delete entries and projects in WalkLedger. Deleting a project deletes its entries from the active ledger. Unused media is removed after it is no longer needed for active exports; temporary or recoverable system copies may persist until cleaned up. Deletion is not a promise of forensic secure erasure.

Deleting the app removes its local data. It does not delete PDFs, backups or other copies you previously exported, nor copies held by recipients, storage providers or device backup services. Manage those separately with the relevant service. Keep a complete backup outside WalkLedger before deleting the app if you want to restore your work later.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. This is separate from the app: WalkLedger does not automatically send project data or diagnostics to support. Please avoid sending sensitive project records or complete backups unless necessary.

We use support correspondence to answer your request and investigate the issue you report. Email providers handle delivery and storage under their own privacy practices. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to address the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information in support correspondence, including access, correction or deletion. Contact daniele@duck.com to ask about those rights. This does not give us access to projects held only on your device.

These support pages

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The page content does not include analytics scripts, advertising or a contact form. GitHub may process connection information, such as an IP address, when delivering the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices. Visiting this website does not give us access to projects stored in WalkLedger.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app or these practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions, contact daniele@duck.com. For help using the app, visit WalkLedger support.
