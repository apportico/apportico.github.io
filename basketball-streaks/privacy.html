Basketball Streaks Privacy Policy

Basketball Streaks saves your records, selected level, and audio preferences on your device. We do not collect personal data through the app. The app has no developer account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Apple handles purchases and restores through the App Store. The app uses Apple’s purchase status to unlock Full Game. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Game Center is optional. If you connect, the game sends leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple. Your scores can be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. See Game Center & Privacy.

If you contact us for support, we use the information you send to respond.
