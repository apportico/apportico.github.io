Basketball Streaks Support

Shooting and streaks

Swipe up from the lower half of the court. Angle your swipe to aim and vary its speed to change the distance. The level intro shows a possible route to the basket. Try Shoot again or swipe up to restart after a miss.

Music and sound effects

Open Levels and scroll below the court list. Music and Sound Effects have independent switches. The game respects your device’s silent mode.

Full Game and restore

The first 20 levels are included. A one-time purchase opens levels 21–120. To restore access, open Levels and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account you used to buy Full Game.

Game Center

Tap the trophy to open Records, connect to Game Center, and see your leaderboards and achievements.

Contact

Email Basketball Streaks Support with your app version, device model, and what happened.
