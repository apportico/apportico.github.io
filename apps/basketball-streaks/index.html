Basketball StreaksSwipe to shoot hoops

How many can you make in a row?

Aim with one thumb and swipe to shoot. Every basket builds your streak. Every miss ends the run.

  • Practice swishes, glass banks, timed shots and floor rebounds through 120 levels at six different courts.
  • Play offline for a personal best or connect to Game Center for three leaderboards and 20 achievements.
  • Start with 20 levels. A one-time Full Game purchase opens levels 21–120.
