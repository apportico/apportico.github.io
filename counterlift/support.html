How to play

When one lift goes up, the other goes down. Tap Pick up when someone is waiting beside their lift, then Exit at their destination. The move target is optional.

Restore Full Game

Open the level picker and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought the unlock. The unlock includes 100 additional levels.

Contact

Email Counterlift support with your app version, device model and what happened.
