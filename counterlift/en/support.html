How to play

When one lift goes up, the other goes down. Tap Pick up when a passenger is waiting beside their lift. Tap Exit when the lift reaches their destination. Finish a puzzle at its move target to earn three stars.

Restore Full Game

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy the unlock. The unlock includes 108 additional levels.

Contact

When contacting support, include the app version, device model, and what happened.
