Counterlift Privacy Policy

You can play without a Counterlift account. Counterlift keeps your progress and preferences on your device. The app has no developer-run game server, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Game Center is optional. When you sign in through Counterlift, the app sends achievement progress and leaderboard scores to Apple. Counterlift keeps a game-specific player ID on your device to separate progress between Game Center players. Other Game Center players can see global leaderboard scores. Use Apple’s Game Center activity sharing settings to choose who can see your achievement progress. Game Center

Apple processes purchases and restores. StoreKit tells Counterlift whether Full Game is unlocked. We do not receive your payment details.
