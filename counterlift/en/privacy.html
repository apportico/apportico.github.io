Counterlift Privacy Policy

Counterlift does not collect personal data from the app. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Progress and sound and haptic settings are stored on your device.

Apple processes purchases and restores. StoreKit tells Counterlift whether Full Game is unlocked. We do not receive your payment details.
