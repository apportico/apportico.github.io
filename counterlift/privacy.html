Counterlift Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026.

Counterlift does not collect personal data from the app. It has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. Puzzle progress and sound and haptic preferences are stored on your device.

Apple processes purchases and restores. StoreKit tells Counterlift whether the Full Game unlock is available to you. We do not receive your payment details. See the Apple privacy policy for how Apple handles account and purchase data.

If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond.
