How to play

Drag a piece on the bench and watch its shadow move. Use the curved arrows to rotate the selected piece. Tap Hint to see where the piece belongs. When a brass mark appears in the lamp track, slide the lamp to the mark.

Restore Full Game

Tap the puzzle counter and choose Restore Purchases in Full Game. Use the Apple Account that bought Full Game. A single purchase unlocks 100 puzzles beyond the 12 free puzzles.
