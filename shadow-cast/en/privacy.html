Shadow Cast Privacy Policy

Shadow Cast keeps your puzzle progress and sound preference on your device. The app has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking. It does not send Apportico personal data.

Apple handles purchases and restores. StoreKit tells the app whether Full Game is unlocked. Apportico does not receive your payment details. Read Apple’s privacy policy for more information.

If you email support, we use the information you send to answer you.
