Privacy Policy

The Great Cheese Heist stores your best star rating for each level and your sound preference on your device. The game has no Apportico account, advertising, analytics or tracking.

If iCloud is enabled, the game uses Apple’s iCloud service to sync your best level ratings across devices signed in to your Apple Account. Apportico cannot access those iCloud saves.

If you use Game Center, the app sends campaign star totals and achievement results to Apple under your Game Center account. Apple also handles Hotel Wings purchases and restores. See Apple's privacy policy for information about these services.

If you email support, Apportico uses your email address and the details you provide to answer your request. Email Apportico with privacy questions.
