The Great Cheese Heist Support

How do I finish a level?

Bring each piece of cheese to a mouse hole. The question-mark button in the game explains each room's obstacles. You'll earn three stars by finishing without losing a life.

How do I restore Hotel Wings?

Open the level picker, choose a locked level and tap the restore button in the purchase panel. Use the Apple Account that originally bought the expansion.

Where are my stars?

Stars are stored on your device. You can also sign in to Game Center from the level picker to post your totals and unlock achievements.

Email Apportico support with your app version, iPhone model and level number. Keep account and payment details private.
