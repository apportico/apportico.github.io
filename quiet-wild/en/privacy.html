The Quiet Wild Privacy Policy

The Quiet Wild keeps your puzzle progress, puzzle times, and preferences on your device. The app has no separate account, ads, third-party analytics, or tracking.

When you opt in to Apple Game Center, the app sends achievement progress and the number of completed animal puzzles to Apple. Puzzle times stay on your device. Read Apple’s Game Center privacy information to learn how Apple handles that data.

Apple handles purchases and restores. StoreKit tells the app which expeditions you can open. Apportico does not receive your payment details. Read Apple’s privacy policy for details about Apple’s services.

If you email support, we use the information you send to respond.
