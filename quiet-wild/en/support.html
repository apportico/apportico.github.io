The Quiet Wild Support

Start a puzzle

Turn the globe to choose an expedition, tap Explore, and pick a sighting. Drag pieces into the board. Use the turn control to rotate a selected piece. The piece mode menu lets you play with all pieces upright.

Restore an expedition

Open the shop from the globe, or tap Explore and Unlock expedition on a locked expedition. Then choose Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Individual packs and All Expeditions are one-time purchases.

Game Center

Open progress from the globe to see your completed puzzles. The Game Center section gives you access to achievements and the leaderboard when signed in to Apple Game Center.

Contact

Email The Quiet Wild support with your app version, device model, and what happened.
