Privacy policy

Daniele Zanni publishes Every Cat. The game has no accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics. The app does not send your coloring progress or drawings to us.

Progress and backups

Coloring progress is stored on your device. Your device backup settings control whether app data is included in device backups. Deleting the app removes its local progress from that device.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes purchases and restores. We do not receive your payment details. When you choose to share a drawing or replay, iOS sends it to the destination you choose. That destination’s privacy policy applies.

Website and support

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information to deliver and protect the website. If you email us, we use your message and contact information to respond and troubleshoot. Contact Every Cat about privacy.
