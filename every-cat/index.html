Every Cat support

Every Cat is an offline color-by-number game for iPhone and iPad.

Color a drawing

Open a collection and choose a drawing. Select a color number and tap the matching regions. Pinch to zoom or use Find region to locate an unpainted area. The app saves your progress automatically. Use Paint, Before, and After to switch views.

Unlock or restore your collection

The first drawing in each collection is playable without purchase. Full Game is a one-time purchase that unlocks the other drawings. To restore it on a device signed in with the original Apple Account, open Help and settings and select Restore Purchases while connected to the internet.

Contact support

Email Every Cat support. Include your app version, device model, and a brief description of the issue.
