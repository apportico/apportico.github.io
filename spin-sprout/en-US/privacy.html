Privacy · Spin Sprout

Your wheels are saved on this device. We do not collect app data or operate an app backend. There is no account, advertising, analytics or runtime AI. Appearance preferences are saved locally. Device backups may include app data. Exporting or sharing sends only the content you choose to the destination you select; that destination has its own privacy practices. Delete a wheel to remove it from the app. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to serve and secure the website. This is separate from the app. If you email support, we receive the message and information you choose to include. Do not send passwords or private backup contents. We use support correspondence to respond to your request. For privacy questions, contact us at the address below.
