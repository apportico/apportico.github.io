Whiteout Courier privacy

Apportico does not run analytics, serve ads, or operate a player account or gameplay server for Whiteout Courier.

On your iPhone

The app saves settings, best times and distance, and unsent Game Center results locally. It uses Apple’s Game Center player identifier on the device to send a queued time to the right account.

Apple services

Apple handles Race Tour payments and purchase restoration. If you sign in to Game Center, the app sends your race scores and achievement progress to Apple. Game Center displays rankings according to your account settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Contact
