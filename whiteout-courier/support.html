Whiteout Courier support

How to steer

Slide left or right on the snow. Read the next gap ahead of the sled. Blue ice changes the steering response, and green gates give a short speed boost.

Race Tour and Restore Purchases

Race Courses includes 12 free races. The one-time Race Tour purchase unlocks another 100. Open Race Courses and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Endless and Game Center

Three envelopes charge a shield that absorbs one crash. In Race with Friends, sign in to Game Center and finish the same course as a friend to compare best times.
