Last Fare Privacy Policy

Last Fare saves your game progress and preferences on your device. It has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking. We do not collect personal data through the game.

The phone, messages, photos, contacts, receipts, and recordings in Last Fare are fictional. The game does not read your own messages, photos, or contacts, and does not make real calls.

Apple handles purchases and restores. The game checks the purchase through StoreKit to unlock the full case. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple’s privacy policy for details.

If you email support, we use the information you send to respond.
