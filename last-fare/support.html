Last Fare Support

Getting started

Swipe up on the phone’s lock screen. Open Messages, Photos, and Voice Memos to look for evidence. Tap Pin when something seems relevant, then open Case Notes to connect your clues or get a hint.

Unlock or restore the full case

Play the first four scenes for free. After the handoff, open Case Notes and tap Unlock full case. This one-time purchase adds three chapters and two endings. Gameplay works offline; buying or restoring uses Apple’s App Store connection.

To restore access, open Case Notes or Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally bought the unlock.

Contact
