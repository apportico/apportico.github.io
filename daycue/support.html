Support · Daycue

Write a cue, choose the days and the daily time window, and Daycue's widget shows it during that window. Add more lines to a cue and the widget shows a different one each day or each hour. Where windows overlap, the one that started most recently shows. At other times the widget shows your "Other times" message.

To add the widget, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Daycue. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen and add Daycue. To keep one cue on a widget all day, touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose a cue under "Always show".

iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so a change can appear a little after the time you set. Daycue is a visual cue, not an alarm or reminder, and does not send notifications.

To move to a new device, use Settings → Export backup, then Settings → Import backup on the new device. Import checks the whole file first and changes nothing if it cannot be read. Settings also keeps recent automatic copies you can restore.
