How to play

Tap a switch to change a route. Each train needs to reach its own station. When more than one train uses a crossing, use the timetable to send them through on different beats. Tap Depart to run your plan. If it fails, Adjust Plan returns the trains to the start and keeps your switch and timing choices.

Restore Full Game

Tap the level counter and scroll to Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that bought Full Game. The purchase adds 100 puzzles to the 12 free puzzles.
