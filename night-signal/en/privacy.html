Night Signal Privacy Policy

Night Signal stores your puzzle progress and sound preference on your device. The game has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking and does not send us personal data.

Apple handles purchases and restores. StoreKit tells the app whether Full Game is unlocked. We do not receive your payment details. SeeApple’s privacy policyfor more information.

Game Center is optional. When you use it, Night Signal sends your achievement progress to Apple.

If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond.
